Section 3

How to Start the Game

Setting up MathKub takes just a few minutes. Follow these four steps to begin.

1

Draw your starting tiles

Each player draws 14 tiles from the face-down pool and keeps them on their rack, hidden from other players.

2

Set up the remaining tiles

All leftover tiles are placed face down at the centre of the table to form the draw pool.

3

Decide who goes first

Each player draws one tile. The player with the highest-numbered tile starts the game.

4

Begin playing

On each turn, place tiles on the board to form a valid equation, or rearrange existing tiles to open new possibilities.

Section 4

Rules of the Game

A small set of clear rules keeps MathKub fair, challenging, and mathematically rigorous.

1

Every equation on the board must be mathematically correct. For example, 3 + 4 = 7 is valid; 3 + 4 = 8 is not.

2

Players may rearrange or extend any equation already on the table, as long as every resulting equation remains valid.

3

Decimal results are not allowed. Division must produce whole numbers — 8 ÷ 4 = 2 is valid, but 7 ÷ 2 is not.

4

A Joker tile can replace any tile — a number, an operator, or an equal sign.

5

If a player cannot play, they draw one tile from the pool and skip that turn.

6

The first player to place all their tiles on the board wins. The game also ends if the pool runs out.

Section 5

Why Is MathKub Unique?

MathKub is a creative blend of strategy, competition and mental arithmetic that stands apart from any other board game.

What Makes It Original?

It merges the tactical depth of Rummikub with the precision of forming correct mathematical equations on every turn.

Why Is It Fun?

Players can modify existing equations and create new opportunities from the board — every move is a creative puzzle.

Skills It Develops

  • Mental calculation
  • Logical thinking
  • Problem solving
  • Strategic planning

How Is It Different?

Traditional Rummikub uses sequences and colour groups. MathKub replaces those with mathematical equations.

Section 7

Educational Purpose

MathKub was designed as a learning tool. Every game session reinforces key cognitive and mathematical skills naturally, through play.

Mental Maths

Repeated arithmetic under mild time pressure sharpens calculation speed and accuracy.

Strategic Thinking

Deciding when to modify existing equations or place new ones requires planning ahead.

Engaging Learning

Embedding mathematics inside a competitive game makes practice genuinely enjoyable.

Learning Through Play

Game-based learning improves retention and motivation in students of all ages.
